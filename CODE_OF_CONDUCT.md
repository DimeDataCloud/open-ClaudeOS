# Code of conduct

We want this to be a place where careful people do careful work together.

- **Be kind and be specific.** Disagree with the idea, not the person. Say what you
  saw, what you expected, and what you would change.
- **Assume good faith, and ask.** Many contributors are working in a second
  language, at odd hours, or on an unfamiliar platform.
- **Keep it welcoming.** No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or
  publishing other people's private information. Sexualised language and imagery
  are not welcome.
- **Security reports are not drama.** Report problems privately (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and expect thanks, not blame.
- **No spam, no astroturfing, no unlabelled AI-generated reviews.** If an AI wrote
  it, say so.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict participation, to
keep to these. To report a concern, use the private "Report a vulnerability"
channel on this repository and mark it as a conduct report, or contact a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.

This is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, v2.1.
